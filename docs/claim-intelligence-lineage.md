# V5.03 — source lineage and independence review

Internal free-only offline API: `analyze_source_lineage(graph, links=(), ownership_hints=(), extractions=(), ephemeral_bodies=None)`. It consumes a typed V5 claim/evidence graph and recomputes V4 duplicate evidence from the original observations and optional typed extraction receipts. Supplied ephemeral body text requires the existing permission/digest checks and is never returned. Graph and extraction receipts are structurally validated, not authenticated. No network, model sampling, new dependencies, persistence, or MCP registry change.

`LineageLink` declares a **claim-specific** dependency between exact source revisions: wire copy, press release, quoted primary source, repost, shared dataset, revision, or other derivation. Requires an existing claim, distinct graph sources, and literal reference wording in a retained, non-locator anchor of the dependent revision. The caller declares the relationship; literal text does not establish its semantics or authenticate the upstream item. All links must connect back to that claim's evidence or origin. Traverse declared links to identify candidate shared upstream revisions; cycles are flagged and never treated as a unique ultimate source. Different claims do not inherit each other's declarations.

`OwnershipHint` records a caller-annotated publisher/owner reference from a checked document excerpt. Publisher reference must match the source's reported label or exact non-aggregator host; owner and publisher wording must appear literally in the excerpt. Shared/conflicting owner labels are review signals, not verified ownership or proof of coordinated reporting. No ownership registry or identity resolution implied.

Per claim, keep origin, support, contradiction, context and insufficient evidence roles separate. Report exact revision IDs on each side, overlapping sides, candidate dependency pairs with referenced link IDs, and V4 direct duplicate evidence. Same URL, host, bare digest, canonical hint, similar headline, substantial copy candidate, declared shared lineage and ownership hints are distinct signals. No transitive merging of copied articles or event identities. Do not infer independence from different hosts, owners, absence of evidence, or no detected pair. Independent-source counts remain null, and no semantic assessment or verdict is produced.

Bounds: existing 80 graph sources and claim limits; 240 lineage links, 80 ownership hints, 20,000 claim/source pair comparisons and existing V4 body/observation limits. Sorted inputs and a full-report fingerprint support deterministic replay. Unknown lineage and unauthenticated declarations remain unknown. The synthetic tests are contract regressions, not an independently labeled real-world independence benchmark. Evaluate shared upstream detection, wire and press-release attribution accuracy, mixed reporting, ownership changes, cyclic and missing provenance, and false independence decisions on held-out labels before claiming production reliability.

Design references: https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/ (derivation, quotation, revision and primary source); https://www.iptc.org/std/NewsML-G2/guidelines/ (creator, provider, credit and source distinction); https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10820/ (trace original sources and corroborate information). Principles only, not formal standards conformance.
