# V5.02: deterministic claim candidate extraction

Experimental free-only offline API. `extract_observation_claims(observations, as_of=...)` accepts up to 80 typed V4 observations, deduplicates exact observation replays, and preserves source revisions. `extract_claim_candidates(as_of=..., sources=..., document_texts=...)` accepts typed graph sources and explicitly permitted supplied document text. Outputs include an immutable V5.01 graph, one candidate or abstention per distinct source, controlled reasons, counts and a full-report fingerprint.

The initial rule grammar deliberately supports only complete English named-subject numerical statements with allowlisted measurement/transaction predicates and units. It preserves exact quantities, signs, approximation/comparison qualifiers, explicit date/period literals, tense, negation and speaker attribution. Allegations, direct attributed quotes and future projections retain distinct modality. A bare headline assertion is a reported claim candidate, not truth. Questions, conditionals, conjunctions, unsupported punctuation/units, ambiguous pronouns, mixed modality, unsupported languages and instruction-like text abstain. No paraphrasing, unit conversion, coreference, translation, scope inference or asserted-date invention. This is a high-abstention baseline, not a comprehensive claim extractor or semantic checkworthiness classifier.

Source text must be complete relative to its supplied representation. Headline rules use the full immutable headline. Documents require caller-established rights, explicit permitted-excerpt retention, a matching full-text hash and at most 512 characters of safe standalone text; longer, hash-only, missing and opinion documents abstain. The supplied representation need not be the full original document, and its origin is not authenticated. Graph validation recomputes deterministic candidate fields from the complete retained anchor and refuses altered fields or cropped evidence. No automatically assigned evidence relations or verdicts.

No network, model sampling, external actions, paid dependencies or persistence. Source instructions are data and never dispatched. Existing caller-annotation contracts remain unchanged. The API is internal and not registered in the MCP runtime.

Evaluation: synthetic regressions measure contract integrity, not actual extraction accuracy. Held-out independent labels must measure faithfulness, atomicity, decontextualization, coverage, modality errors and abstention, including multilingual and nonnumeric claims. Relevant research: Metropolitansky & Larson (2025), https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.348/ ; Ullrich et al. (2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04955 ; Hu et al. (2025), https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.320/ . The rule grammar is not a reproduction of those models or their reported results.
